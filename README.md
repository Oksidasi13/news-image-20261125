# Wednesday Echo — News of 25 November 2026

A one-page newspaper front page summing up Wednesday 25 November 2026 in six verified stories: Baghdad orders the takeover of Kurdish oilfields, Nvidia falls 7% as Congress turns on Huang, the CIA and US intelligence are hit by a cyber breach, Britain sanctions the Houthis and four officials, Real Madrid beat PSV 5–1, and a car bomb wounds 23 in Colombia.

The image is drawn from scratch with Pillow (`draw.py`, no photos or external assets); `main.ts` copies `entry.png` to the output directory. To redraw the image, run `python3 draw.py` — it is deterministic, so repeated runs produce byte-identical output.
